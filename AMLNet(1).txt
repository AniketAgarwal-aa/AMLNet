SECTION 3. DOMAIN-SPECIFIC QUESTION ENGINE AUDIT
========================================================================================
1. Finance & AML Questions:
   - What instruments and transaction types are modeled?
     Synthetic wire transfers and account-to-account payments with fields: timestamp, step,
     source account ID, target account ID, transfer amount (₹ INR), transaction type, and SAR label.
   - Are transactions simulated or live?
     Simulated. Data originates from two deterministic sources: (a) deterministic seed-42 AMLNet
     generator (100 demo accounts, 500 transactions), and (b) IBM AMLSim agent-based synthetic simulation
     across 5 dataset tiers up to 1M vertices and 100M edges.
   - How are transaction costs and market microstructure handled?
     Not applicable; this is retail/commercial banking transaction monitoring, not exchange market microstructure.
   - What regulatory frameworks govern the logic?
     Advisory compliance with FATF guidance on suspicious activity detection and FinCEN SAR filing
     workflows. AMLNet outputs are investigative leads, not legal proof of money laundering.

2. AI/ML Questions:
   - How are training, validation, and test splits isolated?
     Strict inductive splitting: 60% Train, 20% Validation, 20% Held-Out Test. All feature standardization
     (StandardScaler) is fit strictly on training splits. Ground-truth SAR flags and alert IDs are excluded
     from feature matrices to guarantee zero target leakage.
   - How is severe class imbalance (<0.2% positive SAR rate) handled?
     Binary Focal Loss with gamma=2.0 and alpha=0.25 to prevent gradient starvation, combined with
     validation threshold calibration to maximize F1-score rather than assuming a naive 0.5 probability cutoff.

3. Cybersecurity Questions:
   - What authentication model protects sensitive actions?
     FIDO2 WebAuthn Passkeys backed by platform authenticators (Touch ID, Face ID, Windows Hello) and
     X-Admin-Key API gating. Zero biometric data ever touches the server; only cryptographic signatures
     are verified via public-key cryptography.

SECTION 4. UNIVERSAL PROJECT IDENTITY & OVERVIEW
========================================================================================
Project Name: AMLNet 2.0 (v2.2.3)
Executive Summary:
AMLNet is an advanced, evidence-based anti-money laundering analytics platform. It addresses the fundamental
flaw of conventional AML systems—unmanageable false positive rates and inability to uncover multi-hop fund
dispersal—by combining topological graph representation learning with interpretable gradient-boosted trees
and FIDO2 biometric security.

Technical Summary:
The system models bank accounts as graph nodes V and transactions as directed multigraph edges E. For every
account, the engine extracts graph topological features (in-degree, out-degree, flow concentration, degree
ratios, transaction velocity, amount variance). A two-stage analytical pipeline evaluates candidates:
1. Stage-1 Candidate Filtering (High Recall): Deep Graph Neural Networks (GraphSAGE, GAT, GCN) achieve
   99.5%–99.8% recall on suspicious accounts.
2. Stage-2 Precision Scoring & Ranking: Gradient-boosted decision trees (HistGradientBoosting) maximize
   precision (74.5%) and F1 (0.763) on dense graph features.
3. Rule-Based Forensic Evidence: Real-time pattern engines detect topological cycles, rapid fund hops,
   fan-in concentration, and fan-out structuring.
4. Composite Risk Engine: Combines GNN probabilities and heuristic rule weights into an explainable 0–100 score.

SECTION 14. AI, MACHINE LEARNING & 30-MODEL BENCHMARK AUDIT
========================================================================================
1. Model Architectures Evaluated:
   - GraphSAGE (PyG): Mean neighborhood aggregator with 2 layers (dim 64 -> 32).
   - GAT (PyG): Graph Attention Network with 4 attention heads and ELU activations.
   - GCN (PyG): Two-layer spectral graph convolution network with dropout 0.2.
